# Project 01: Inventory & Demand Planning Analytics

## Business Problem

The company wants to improve inventory availability while
reducing excess inventory, stockouts, and supply-chain costs.

## Objectives

1. Analyze historical demand and inventory performance.
2. Identify high-value and slow-moving inventory.
3. Evaluate stockout and fulfillment performance.
4. Analyze supplier lead-time performance.
5. Calculate inventory optimization metrics.
6. Analyze demand patterns and seasonality.
7. Develop and evaluate demand forecasting models.
8. Generate actionable supply-chain insights.
